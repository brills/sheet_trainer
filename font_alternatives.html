Typography Comparison — 'M' (Major) vs 'm' (minor) Clarity

Notice why Cinzel causes issues (it is a small-caps titling font with no true lowercase 'm') and compare alternative typefaces below.

The Cinzel Issue: In Cinzel, lowercase m is rendered as a miniature uppercase ᴍ. In chord symbols, C Maj and C m or Cmaj7 and Cm7 look virtually identical because both use the capital M glyph structure.
